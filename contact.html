Patient Protocols

Pre-Appointment Preparation & FAQ

Please review these clinical recommendations prior to arriving for any injectable or laser session.

48 Hours Before

Avoid NSAIDs (aspirin, ibuprofen), fish oils, and alcohol to drastically minimize microscopic bruising and hematoma formation.

Day of Procedure

Arrive with a clean, makeup-free face. Our clinical staff will complete an antiseptic wash and apply physician-grade numbing cream.

Cancellation Policy

We request 48 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Appointments cancelled under 24 hours forfeit consultation holding fee.
