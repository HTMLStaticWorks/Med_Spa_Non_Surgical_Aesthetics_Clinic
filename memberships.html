Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I cannot visit one month?

Your monthly dues roll over into your aesthetic banking wallet indefinitely. You can combine banked credits toward a major laser protocol or filler rejuvenation at any time.

Is there a minimum commitment contract?

We require a 3-month initial membership so your skin experiences true compounding cellular remodeling. After 3 months, you may pause or cancel with 14 days written notice.

Can I transfer credits to a friend or partner?

Yes. Sculpt and Haute Prestige members may allocate up to 2 treatment sessions annually to immediate family members or guests.
