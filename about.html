Our Heritage

The Antidote to the Fast-Food Aesthetic Clinic

Aurelia was created to restore elegance, medical gravitas, and surgical precision to aesthetic medicine. We operate without commission incentives, rush quotas, or generic treatment menus.

Every protocol begins with 45 minutes of comprehensive micro-anatomical mapping under the direct guidance of Dr. Aurelia Vance and our senior injector faculty.

Founding Story

Why We Started: Redefining Medical Aesthetics

In an era when aesthetic medicine was rapidly becoming commercialized—with rushed 15-minute appointments, commission-driven upselling, and unsupervised injectors—Dr. Aurelia Vance founded Aurelia to offer an uncompromising alternative.

Having trained as a reconstructive facial plastic surgeon at Johns Hopkins, Dr. Vance observed that patients were increasingly suffering from unnatural, over-filled distortions caused by superficial techniques. Aurelia was built as a physician-led sanctuary where time is luxury, safety is non-negotiable, and every enhancement honors your innate facial harmony.
